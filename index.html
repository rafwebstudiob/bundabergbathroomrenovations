Renovating a bathroom in Bundaberg

Bundaberg's older inner suburbs have bathrooms that are often small and were built before current waterproofing standards, including timber Queenslanders in Bundaberg West and Kepnock. Post-war bathrooms in Norville, Walkervale, Svensson Heights and Avoca are common candidates for a full renovation or a walk-in shower.

Older wet areas can hide asbestos-containing sheeting and failed waterproofing, which must be handled safely. In Queensland, check that the contractor holds a QBCC licence that covers the work, and that waterproofing, plumbing and electrical work is done by suitably licensed trades.

Does the waterproofer need a licence?

In Queensland, wet-area waterproofing must meet the National Construction Code and AS 3740, and should be done by a suitably licensed contractor. Ask for a certificate. See our waterproofing guide.

What licence should the renovator have?

In Queensland, the QBCC says work over $3,300 needs a written contract and home warranty cover, with deposits capped at 10% (under $20,000) or 5% ($20,000 or more).
